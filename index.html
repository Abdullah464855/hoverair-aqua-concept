Water-first

This drone was not adapted to water. It was born in it.

The orange foam ring is buoyancy, prop protection and visibility on dark water in one piece. The body is non-foldable on purpose — folding joints are leak points. Components are corrosion-resistant, and the lens is hydrophobic and self-heating, so droplets and fog do not ruin the shot.

  • + Floats and takes off from the surface — no launch pad, no dry hand, no beach run-up
  • + Turtle Flip self-righting — flipped by a wave, it rights itself and hovers, waiting
  • + Dead battery? It lands softly on the surface and floats — you paddle to it, you do not dive for it

Lighthouse

You wear the controller on your arm. Then you forget it exists.

The Lighthouse wearable strapped to your arm is the link — RTK-assisted tracking keeps the drone locked on you even when you turn away, and it handles takeoff, landing, recording, mode selection and remote recall from your wrist. A surfer cannot hold a controller and a kayaker cannot clip on a phone, so this is what makes the solo water shot possible.

RTK-assisted tracking Takeoff from your wrist 15+ flight modes Follows at up to 55 km/h

15+ flight modes — Follow, Orbit, Bird's Eye, plus dedicated Paddle, Kayak, Foil and Snorkel modes, tracking at up to 55 km/h.

Camera

4K at 100 frames a second — because water only looks fast in slow motion.

12MP stills with HDR from a 1/1.28-inch sensor, 4K/100fps slow motion, 4K/60fps HDR for bright reflections off the surface, 10-bit H-Log for creators who grade, and vertical shooting for Reels and TikTok. SmoothCapture 3.0 pairs a 1-axis mechanical gimbal with electronic stabilisation and horizon levelling.

128GB internal storage, no microSD slot — plenty for a day on the water, but when it fills you transfer before you fly again.

The honest section

The price, the battery and the missing sensor — said out loud.

+01

23 minutes is the claim

Reviewers tracking actively in strong wind measured 10–13 minutes, so plan short shoots and spare batteries.

+02

It follows you. It does not dodge

Tracking is positional (ShadowTrack), not obstacle avoidance. It belongs over open water, not through trees or near cliffs.

+03

£1,129, and not sold in the US

Roughly four times a Neo 2, and reported as not currently available in the US (FCC/regulatory), so no US delivery promises.

After salt water, rinse and dry it — every time. The battery is only fully waterproof while it sits in the drone.
